About ShelfSwap

ShelfSwap started in our hostel when a few of us realised we were all buying the same books every semester.

No money changes hands. You bring a book, you take a book.

How it works

  1. Post the book name and its condition on the group.
  2. We add it to the list for the week.
  3. Meet the other person outside the mess on Saturday evening.
